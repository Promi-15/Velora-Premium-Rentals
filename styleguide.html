Glass vs Solid Rules

Glass Elements (40-55% white, 20px blur)

Used STRICTLY on floating elements over imagery:

  • Floating Navbar Pill
  • Hero Booking Bar
  • Callout chips with fine leader lines
  • "Watch the story" video thumbnail chip
  • Floating pill labels on arch frames

Solid Elements

All primary structural components:

  • White & soft gray section canvases
  • Solid Ink Black pill buttons with separate circular arrow buttons
  • Hairline dividers (1px, 8% ink)
  • Dark ink pricing cards and footer
