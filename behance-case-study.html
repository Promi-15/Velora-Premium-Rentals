Slide 02 • Overview & Personas

Solving Dhaka's High-End Mobility Friction

Traditional car rental in Dhaka suffers from unvetted drivers, hidden surcharges, lack of airport VIP protocols, and outdated phone-only bookings. Velora Drive bridges this gap with instant transparent BDT pricing and verified executive chauffeurs.

Corporate Traveller (30-45)

Airport Pickup & Executive Transit

Pain point: Unpunctual drivers, missing VAT receipts. Solution: Real-time airport flight tracking & monthly corporate invoicing.

Wedding Client (25-40)

Prestige Ceremonial Fleet

Pain point: Poor car aesthetics & hidden costs. Solution: High-spec Prados & Mercedes with bespoke floral deco packages.

International Visitor (22-40)

Expat & Tourist Mobility

Pain point: Language barrier & route chaos. Solution: English-fluent chauffeurs, GPS-monitored routes, 24/7 WhatsApp concierge.

Velora Drive — Premium Car Rental & Chauffeur Landing Page
Velora Drive is a concept landing page for a premium car rental and chauffeur service in Dhaka. The goal was to help visitors search and book a car in under a minute, while building trust through clear pricing, verified drivers, and real reviews. Designed with a dark luxury aesthetic, responsive for desktop and mobile.
